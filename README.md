# 🎣 Pechadophin

**Version V0.6 · CASE UPDATE**

🇫🇷 [Lire en français](README.fr.md)

<h2 align="center">▶️ <a href="https://pechadophin.vercel.app/">PLAY NOW: pechadophin.vercel.app</a></h2>

A pixel-art platformer you play right in your browser: you are a fisherman fighting dolphins, a shark demon, a giant octopus, a jellyfish and a sand castle, in a world randomly generated every run.

The whole game fits in a single HTML file, with no installation and no dependencies (apart from the Google Fonts "Press Start 2P" font).

## ▶️ Play

- Online: **[https://pechadophin.vercel.app/](https://pechadophin.vercel.app/)**
- Locally: download the repository and open `index.html` in your browser.

## 🎮 Controls

| Action | Keyboard / mouse | Tablet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | `A` / `D` (or `Q` / `D` on AZERTY) or `←` / `→` | Left joystick |
| Jump | `W` / `Z`, `↑` or `Space` | JUMP button |
| Aim | Mouse | Right joystick (aim assist) |
| Fish | Left click | FISH button |
| Pause | `Esc` or `P` | ⏸ button |

## ✨ Content

- Eleven weapons, shown in the WEAPONS menu, almost all with their own skill tree: an upgradable fishing rod, an infernal trident (unlocked on the beach), daggers that attack in a dash (in the red chest of the swamps), the Number 6 weapon (secret swamp boss), the ice scepter (dropped by the Eskimo), the tarot deck, the harpoon, the katana, the boomerang, the tentacles sprouting from your back, and the scythe (4-hit combo, poison)
- TOP 10 leaderboard for Anarchy mode
- Blue dolphins, pink (flying) ones and red ones (fireball shooters), sand worms and diving seagulls on the beach
- A laser-firing shark demon, accompanied by a blood-red night
- An arena with a giant octopus on each side of the start
- The beach: a sand zone with tunnels, worms, a checkpoint and palm trees that act as platforms, a flying boss (the jellyfish, white lasers and rotating tentacles) and the sand castle (sand waves, water bucket to aim at)
- The swamps: dark grass, puddles, ruined towers used as platforms, mini zombies, mini cows and the giant cow (rolls and milk spits)
- Chests to open along the way to win gold
- Three game modes: Classic, Anarchy (endless bosses, a random zone after each arena boss, a sky that changes with every shark) and Speedrun (unlocked after your first rebirth: beat King Cacamille 2 against the clock, with a weapon of your choice already maxed out, no checkpoints)
- Game available in French or English (flag button)
- Sign in with a Google account and automatic cloud save to your Google Drive (see [privacy.html](privacy.html))
- Collab with the game RNG∞: an RNG∞ skin to unlock with the same Google account
- Merchants in the middle of a run to upgrade your equipped weapon
- A crate system: 36 skins to unlock through fragments in the Hero Crate and the Villain Crate (animated roulette), including the Épouvofodille scarecrow
- A shop: unlocked / locked skins, a premium category (Clown, Épouvofodille, RNG∞), upgrades (gold) and red souls
- New main menu, pause menu, local save of your best score and the shop, and a save reset button (double confirmation)
